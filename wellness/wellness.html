The Estate

A whole estate, held for stillness

Ses Garites sits inside the medieval walls of Pals, minutes from the Mediterranean — stone vaults, exposed beams, and terracotta floors that have absorbed five centuries of quiet.

For retreat leaders and small groups, the estate is available only as a whole. No shared spaces, no passing strangers — just your group, the garden, and the stillness of an old house.

Room to spread out. Silence when you need it. A held space, not a hired hall.

A Day at Ses Garites

Structured enough to hold, loose enough to breathe

A typical rhythm for a retreat group — shaped around your practice, not the other way round.

Morning7 – 10am

Movement & stillness

Yoga or meditation as the light comes up over the meadow, followed by a slow breakfast on the terrace with produce from the local market.

Midday10am – 4pm

Open studio time

Painting on the terrace, writing in the library or a quiet room, or simply time by the pool. The estate is large enough that no two people need to keep the same pace.

Evening5 – 10pm

Gathering

A second sitting or group session as the light softens, then a long dinner in the vaulted dining hall — candlelight, conversation, and nowhere else to be.
